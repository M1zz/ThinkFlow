Privacy Policy

ThinkFlow · Effective date: June 11, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

In short: ThinkFlow does not collect personal information.
The thoughts and notes you write are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere else. The only things that reach the developer are anonymous usage statistics and error reports that can’t be traced back to you, and feedback you choose to send yourself.

1. Personal information we collect

ThinkFlow does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up, the developer runs no servers, and we don’t collect information such as your name, email address, location or device identifiers. The only exceptions are the anonymous usage statistics described in section 3 and the feedback described in section 4.

2. How your data is stored

Everything you create in the app (thoughts, notes, bridge sentences, attached references and thinking-time records) is stored only in the following places:

  • The app’s storage on your device
  • A shared App Group container on the same device, used to display widgets

This data is never sent over the network, and no one, including the developer, can access it.

3. Anonymous usage statistics and diagnostics

To understand how many people use the app and to fix bugs, the app sends the following anonymous statistics to Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Only the developer can read them.

  • A random ID created at installation (not linked to your device, your Apple Account or any other app, and it changes if you reinstall)
  • App version, iOS version, and region and language settings
  • How many times and on which days you opened the app, the date you installed it and the number of days since installation
  • Diagnostic reports when the app freezes or quits unexpectedly (app version, iOS version, device model and technical details of the error)

These never include your name, contact details, location, or the content of your thoughts and notes. They are sent only while you are signed in to iCloud. Diagnostic reports are passed to the app by Apple’s standard diagnostics framework (MetricKit), and depending on your settings under Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, they may not be generated at all.

4. Sending feedback (only when you choose to)

If you respond to the satisfaction question the app occasionally asks by choosing what could be better and writing feedback, the category you chose, what you wrote, and your app version, iOS version, device model and language setting are delivered to the developer through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Your name or email address is included only if you enter it yourself to get a reply. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and feedback you send is used only to reply to you and to improve the app.

5. Third parties and external services

ThinkFlow does not use third-party services such as advertising SDKs, external analytics services or social login, and does not share any information with third parties. iCloud, which is used for the anonymous usage statistics and feedback, is operated by Apple, and Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to it.

6. Backing up and deleting your data

  • You can back up all of your data yourself as a Markdown or JSON file with the in-app Export feature.
  • When you delete the app, all data stored on your device is deleted with it immediately.
  • Depending on your device’s iCloud Backup settings, app data may be included in your own iCloud device backup. This is standard iOS behavior, and the developer has no access to it.

7. Children’s privacy

Because ThinkFlow does not collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from children either. The anonymous usage statistics in section 3 contain nothing that could reveal who is using the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a new feature changes how data is handled), we will post the update on this page and revise the effective date.
